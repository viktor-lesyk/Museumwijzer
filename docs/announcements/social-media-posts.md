## 3. LinkedIn (Civic Tech, Open Data & Social Inclusion)

### Option 3A: English (Recommended for broader reach)

```text
Culture connects people — but only if people can easily discover what’s out there. 🏛️

The Netherlands has an inspiring cultural inclusion programme called "Welkom in het Museum" (organised by VriendenLoterij, VluchtelingenWerk Nederland, and Het Cultuurfonds), granting newcomers and refugees free access to over 200 museums.

As someone who experienced this programme firsthand, I noticed that while the opportunity was generous, navigating a flat directory made it difficult for newcomers to know what was nearby, whether an exhibition was suitable for families, or which cultural institutions had high visitor ratings.

To solve this, I built and launched Museumwijzer:
🔗 https://museumwijzer.vlesyk.com/en/
💻 Open Source: https://github.com/viktor-lesyk/Museumwijzer

What went into building it:
⚡ Performance & Privacy First: Built with Astro and static-first architecture. Zero cookies, zero user-tracking scripts, and completely serverless.
📍 Local Discovery: Integrated Dutch open geodata (PDOK / Kadaster) with client-side distance calculation — users can search by postal code or browser GPS without transmitting location data to a server.
⭐ Context & Enrichment: Filterable by verified Google ratings, themes (Art, History, Castles, Science, Family), and ticket price differentials.
🌐 Trilingual: Native Dutch, English, and Ukrainian localisations to eliminate language barriers.
🔗 Official Funnel: Every museum page guides users directly to the official ticket invitation portal and official museum ticketing desks.

A huge thank you to the organisations making cultural access possible in the Netherlands. If you work with newcomers, culture, or civic tech, I would love to hear your thoughts!

#CivicTech #OpenData #WebDevelopment #Accessibility #SocialInclusion #OpenSource #Netherlands
```
